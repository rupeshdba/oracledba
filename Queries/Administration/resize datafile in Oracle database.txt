You can resize any datafile in the database whose size is less than 31 GB.

alter database datafile &id resize &size_in_GB;

e.g.

alter database datafile 5 resize 10g;

OR

alter database datafile '/u01/app/TEST/oradata/test01.dbf' resize 10g;
